Juan Pablo Durango

Biologist | Herpetologist | Bioacoustician

I am a Colombian Biologist and Herpetologist from EAFIT University. I am passionate about the conservation of Neotropical amphibians and reptiles, with emphasis on the taxonomy, natural history, and bioacoustics of Colombian anurans.

I have experience in environmental consulting, molecular techniques (DNA extraction, PCR, Oxford Nanopore sequencing), and field surveys in remote, high-biodiversity areas. I am committed to scientific outreach and community-based conservation.

📍 Medellín, Colombia

📸 Fieldwork in Chocó, Colombia

Research interests

  • Taxonomy and systematics of amphibians and reptiles
  • Natural history and conservation of amphibians and reptiles
  • Animal comunication and Bioacoustics
  • DNA barcoding and third-generation sequencing (Oxford Nanopore)
  • Science outreach with local communities
